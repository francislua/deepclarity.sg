D6 — Strategic HR

Compensation Strategy

Section D · Questions Q79–Q85

If you would like to benchmark pay against actual market data, this typically involves a specialist or data vendor, happy to point you in the right direction if useful. The questions below are about your current approach, not about getting that data right now.

Q79

Which of these best describes how pay is generally decided? (Select all that apply.)

Q80

Is any part of compensation tied to performance, goals, or results?

Q81

Does the practice offer any commission, bonus, profit-sharing, or skills-based pay (e.g. skills allowances)?

Q82

If you selected 'researched or benchmarked' or 'believe is market rate' above, how would you describe that approach further?

Q83

How were current pay levels actually arrived at, and is there a sense of whether pay is fair and consistent across similar roles?

Q84

If yes to commission, bonus, profit-sharing, or skills-based pay, how is this structured, and for which roles?

Q85

Beyond pay, are flexible work arrangements, staff eyewear discounts, recognition (formal or informal), or general work environment actively considered as part of attracting and retaining staff, or are these treated separately, disconnected from pay strategy?
