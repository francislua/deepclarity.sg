Location and Catchment

Section A · Questions Q13–Q14

Q13

Where is your business located? How would you describe the surrounding area and customer catchment?

Consider: type of location (HDB heartland, suburban mall, medical or hospital cluster, CBD or office district, shophouse or standalone), the demographic profile of the area, and foot traffic patterns. Is your current location working for the type of business you want to run, does the catchment match the customers you are trying to serve?

Q14

If you have more than one outlet, briefly describe each location and whether the same business approach works across all of them, or whether each location serves a meaningfully different customer base.
