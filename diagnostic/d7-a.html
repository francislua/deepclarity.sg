How your practice is organised and how your team works together shapes everything from day-to-day operations to your ability to grow. There are no right answers here, we are trying to understand how things currently work in practice.

Q93

How is your practice currently organised in terms of the people and roles that exist? Describe the different groups, functions, or types of work that exist, who does what, and how people relate to each other day-to-day.

Q94

When you think about how your team works together, what does that look like in practice? What does each group or function need to deliver, and how well is that happening?
