About Francis Lua

Experience shaped across businesses, functions and markets.

I founded DeepClarity after over two decades working with management teams across Asia Pacific at Microsoft, TE Connectivity and Johnson & Johnson. Those roles taught me that important business decisions rarely belong to one function. Their effects move through leadership, operations, technology, roles, relationships and the everyday choices people make at work.

Francis Lua Founder, DeepClarity

Why I look at decisions from more than one perspective

Throughout my corporate career, I worked alongside business leaders during periods of growth, organisational change and shifting commercial priorities. I saw how the same decision could look entirely different depending on where someone sat in the organisation.

A commercially attractive choice could create operational strain. A sound technology decision could leave ownership unclear. A carefully designed people initiative could have little effect if it was disconnected from how the business made decisions and allocated resources.

My contribution increasingly became one of helping leaders connect these perspectives. That involved understanding the commercial purpose, listening closely to the people affected, identifying the assumptions behind different views and helping management teams work through the implications.

I founded DeepClarity to bring that way of working to owners and senior leaders facing consequential decisions. I contribute an independent perspective, informed by experience inside large and complex organisations, while remaining attentive to the particular history and realities of each client.

My work combines business understanding, organisation development, systems thinking and an applied coaching practice. I hold a Master of Science in Organization Development from Pepperdine University.

"My role is to help leaders see the connections that are difficult to see from within one function, one role or one version of the problem."
