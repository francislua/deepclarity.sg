New Leader Assimilation

Pre-work survey

This survey is pre-work for the New Leader Assimilation session. It provides an opportunity to share your perspectives, expectations and questions to help your new leader and team work effectively together.

Please respond openly and constructively. All responses will be treated as confidential. The DeepClarity facilitator will be able to identify individual respondents and review their answers to prepare for the session.

During the session, the facilitator will share themes drawn from the responses. Any individual comments used will be anonymised, with names and identifying details removed or paraphrased to reduce the risk of identification. Please bear in mind that distinctive experiences or circumstances may still be recognisable to others.

All 14 questions are required. Your answers save automatically as you go, so you can stop at any point and come back later by signing in again. Once you submit, your answers are final and cannot be changed.

If you do not yet have a view on a question, you may say so. Please keep your sign-in details private.
