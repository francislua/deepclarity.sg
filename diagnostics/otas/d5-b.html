Q54

For part-time or freelance staff, do they have separate written agreements that reflect their specific arrangement? For example, a part-time employment contract or a contractor agreement.

If you use freelance optometrists on a regular basis, it is worth checking that the arrangement is correctly documented. This is an area where an employment lawyer or HR specialist can provide a straightforward review.
