Operational Technology Foundation

Section A · Questions Q37–Q41

Gaps identified in this section may be addressable through the Productivity Solutions Grant (PSG), which co-funds approved technology adoption for Singapore SMEs. Your DeepClarity consultant will flag relevant opportunities during your discussion.

Q37

What system do you currently use to manage sales transactions and basic business records?

If separate systems, describe them:

How long have you been using your current system, and how well does it meet your needs?

Q38

How do you manage patient and customer records, prescriptions, examination history, contact lens details, and recall information?

Is this data consistently captured by all staff across all transactions, or does quality vary?

Q39

How do you currently manage appointment booking and patient recall?

Q40

How do you manage frame and lens inventory?

Do you know your current stock levels and slow-moving items at any point in time, or only when you do a manual count?

Q41

How consistently is data captured across your business, across different staff members, different transaction types, and (if applicable) different outlets?
