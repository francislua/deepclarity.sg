Sourcing and Supplier Strategy

Section D · Questions Q29–Q32

These questions are about how your sourcing decisions connect to your business positioning. There are no right or wrong answers, the diagnostic looks for coherence between what you carry, where you source it, and the customers you are trying to serve.
