D5 — HR Operations

Employment Documentation

Section A · Questions Q49–Q52

This section covers the operational side of managing your people, documentation, payroll, leave, and the basics of how people join your practice. Getting the operational foundations right creates the conditions for everything else to work. If this section surfaces gaps, we will help you understand what needs attention and who can help, and we will sequence the rest of the work accordingly. Some questions here may point you towards a specialist, an employment lawyer, HR consultant, payroll provider, or insurer. That is entirely normal. Part of what this diagnostic does is help you see where to direct your attention.

Q49

Do all staff in your practice have a signed written employment contract? This includes full-time, part-time, and any regular casual staff.

Q50

Does your practice have documented workplace policies that staff can refer to? For example: conduct expectations, leave procedures, grievance or complaint process, disciplinary procedure.

Q51

Do you have any Workplace Health and Safety (WHS) documentation in place? When was it last reviewed?

If you are unsure about your WHS obligations, a WHS specialist can help. This is a referral we can support you with.

Q52

Is any part of your workforce covered by a union agreement? If yes, are you confident the relevant documentation obligations are being met?
